Our Range

Eight products. One quarry. Every specification you need.

Every product is crushed and graded on-site at our own Piliyandala plant, so what you order is exactly what's delivered — no re-sold or blended stock.

01

6/4 Rubble

Large graded rubble for foundations, hardcore filling, and heavy-duty base layers on major construction sites.

Foundations · Filling
02

3/4 Metal

The standard structural grade — reinforced concrete slabs, columns, beams, and general RCC works.

RCC · Slabs · Columns
03

1/4 Metal

Finer aggregate for precast units, thin slabs, plastering backing, and finishing-grade concrete work.

Precast · Fine Finish
04

Dust

Fine crusher dust for plastering, block-work jointing, and as a workable sand substitute in mixes.

Plastering · Block Work
05

Chips

Uniform decorative and structural chips for exposed aggregate finishes, gardens, and precast detailing.

Exposed Finish · Precast
06

ABC Aggregate

All-in-Ballast-Crusher-run for road sub-base, compacted fill, and pavement construction.

Road Base · Compaction
07

Inch Metal

Versatile 25mm crushed metal — drainage layers, retaining-wall back-fill, and gabion fill.

Drainage · Back-fill
08

Mixture

Pre-blended chips and dust for concrete mixes and plastering — one delivery, ready to use.

Ready-Mix Blend

How It Works

From rock face to your site, in four steps.

01

Extract

Granite is blasted and broken at our own quarry face using hydraulic breakers and excavators.

02

Crush

Raw rock is fed through our crushing plant and reduced to the required particle size.

03

Grade

Screened and sorted into our eight product grades — from 6/4 Rubble down to fine Dust.

04

Deliver

Loaded straight onto our own fleet and driven directly to your construction site.
